# 💫 About Me

Hi, I'm **Abhinav Gupta** — known online as **EgoisticCoderX**. I'm 14, based in Kolkata, India, and I build AI/ML systems and full-stack applications that solve real problems.

Currently building **Dokai** — a medical AI platform using MedGemma 4B with multimodal perception, clinical reasoning, and voice I/O — and **StudyMate AI**, an adaptive study assistant for Indian ICSE/CBSE students powered by Neo4j behavioral graphs and spaced repetition.

My core focus is AI/ML systems engineering and embedded hardware. I care about technical rigor over demos — things that actually work in production, not just in a notebook.

Targeting **AI/ML Research Engineering** long-term. The gap between where I am and where I want to be is fuel.

Open to collaborations, research discussions, and feedback on any of my builds.
